from 4 to 64 bits (16x wider): ripple delay x12.6, area x19.0;  tool '+' delay x12.5, area x19.0;  Kogge-Stone delay x3.9, area x34.6.
at 64 bits Kogge-Stone is 3.2x faster than the ripple chain and 1.8x its area.
OBSERVATION: Yosys's default flow turned '+' into a ripple chain (the '+' and ripple columns agree to within rounding). A faster adder has to be asked for or written; the tool does not pick one on its own here.
a ripple chain's delay is proportional to its width (the carry passes through every stage, Chapter 1); a prefix adder's grows with log2(width), at the price of area.
