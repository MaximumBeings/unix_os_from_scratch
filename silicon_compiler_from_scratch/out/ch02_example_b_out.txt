== 1. derived: the width of a sum of K products, worst case |p| = 128*128 = 16384 = 2^14
   K products need ceil(log2(K * 2^14 + 1)) + 1 bits (the +1 is the sign). 32 bits hold 2^31 - 1 = 2147483647.
   K =       1: worst-case sum       16384  needs 16 bits  fits in 32
   K =      16: worst-case sum      262144  needs 20 bits  fits in 32
   K =     256: worst-case sum     4194304  needs 24 bits  fits in 32
   K =    4096: worst-case sum    67108864  needs 28 bits  fits in 32
   K =   65536: worst-case sum  1073741824  needs 32 bits  fits in 32
   K =  131071: worst-case sum  2147467264  needs 32 bits  fits in 32
   K =  131072: worst-case sum  2147483648  needs 33 bits  DOES NOT FIT
   so the largest K that always fits is floor((2^31 - 1) / 16384) = 131071; the 131072nd worst-case product is the first that overflows

== 3. measured: a REAL dot product of random int8 vectors, seed 5, 2000 trials per length (200 for the two longest)
   length K | largest |sum| seen | worst case K*16384 | ratio    | any overflow of 32 bits?
         64 |            147731 |            1048576 | 0.14089 | no
        256 |            327991 |            4194304 | 0.07820 | no
       1024 |            681492 |           16777216 | 0.04062 | no
       4096 |           1428317 |           67108864 | 0.02128 | no
      16384 |           2039896 |          268435456 | 0.00760 | no
      65536 |           4870015 |         1073741824 | 0.00454 | no
   Random data grows like sqrt(K), the worst case like K: the typical sum stays about 1/sqrt(K) of the bound. Overflow needs ADVERSARIAL or very structured data;
   this is why 32-bit accumulators are the industry norm for int8, and why the MAC still has to define what happens (a rare event in a safety-critical system is still an event).
