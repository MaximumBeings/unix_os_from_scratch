== 3. what Capra refuses, with the exact message and the fix
  a product with inner dimension 65
      message: matmul node 2: inner dimension 65 exceeds the matrix unit's limit of 64
      fix: split the contraction into pieces of at most 64 (the hardware has no int32 accumulate instruction), or reduce the width
  a relu that is not directly after a matmul
      message: relu on node 1 (add) cannot be fused into a requantizer
      fix: put the relu directly after a matmul that has no other consumer (the compiler fuses it into the requantizer)
  matrix shapes that do not match
      message: matmul shapes (2, 3) x (4, 4)
      fix: check the shapes; transpose_b=True multiplies by the transpose of the second operand
  tensors too big for the 4,096-word scratchpad (a 64 x 64 weight is the whole of it)
      message: scratchpad is full: cannot place a buffer of 4096 words (free: 3584 words in 1 pieces)
      fix: use smaller tensors or fewer live at once (the allocator already reuses dead buffers); a bigger model must be split into pieces that fit
